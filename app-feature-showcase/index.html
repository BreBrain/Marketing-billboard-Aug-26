App feature showcase: how much can you show at once

One app at the centre, its features arranged around it. Sibling to the OOH moodboard, which studied how brands claim breadth. This one studies how they show it, and answers the question that one left open: how many features can sit around a product before it stops reading. Every entry records a feature count and whether it survives a three second look.
