# Pura moodboards

Two design research collections on one problem: **how a product with many services communicates that breadth in a single image**, without reading as clutter or as burden.

Built for Pura (PureHealth), which has seven service areas that have to land as one coherent offer.

**Live site:** enable GitHub Pages on this repo, then open the Pages URL.

| Collection | What it is | Count |
|---|---|---|
| [`app-feature-showcase/`](app-feature-showcase/) | One app at the centre, features arranged around it. Radial rings, exploded views, isometric worlds, service grids | 50 |
| [`ooh-superapp/`](ooh-superapp/) | Billboards claiming breadth, read at roadside speed | 15 |

Both are single page apps: filterable card grids with a detail drawer. No build step, no dependencies, no server. Open `index.html` and it works.

## The findings

**Feature showcases.** Nothing below ten features failed to read. Above ten, one structure carries almost everything: **the grid**. Revolut's Hub screen holds twenty services and still reads, because a grid is one object the eye takes in whole. Seventeen ungrouped spokes are seventeen separate things, and they do not. Grouping does the same job by another route: Paytm splits twelve into two branches of six, Rappi hangs twenty sub brands off eight ring segments.

**Out of home.** The strongest breadth communication in the whole set contains no app screen at all: Uber's "Go anywhere. Get anything." flanked by two columns of service words. Careem owns pure assertion in the GCC with three words, "The everything app." The message-left / app-screen-right layout turns out to live mostly in agency mockups and stock templates rather than on real streets.

**The gap.** Every breadth demonstration found across both collections is commerce. None is health.

## Method

Sourced with Firecrawl image search, filtered on size and shape, then filtered twice by eye: a contact sheet triage pass, then a full resolution QA pass. The second pass is not optional. It caught AI generated fakes that were completely convincing at thumbnail size, including a billboard reading "Mobile Roseting" and "GET IT IS Google Play", and an "ecosystem strategy" captioned "Data Flows to trasse NeuCoins flow".
