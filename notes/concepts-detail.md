# Ten Pura OOH concepts

Five billboards and five digital verticals. Each one carries the idea, the copy, the placement and the layout, and each descends from a specific reference in the 80 entry moodboard rather than from taste.

Every asset resolves on the locked tagline: **Pura knows your body like you do.**

---

## Billboards, 21:9

### B1  The Container

**Idea.** A single open case holds everything Pura does, as real objects rather than icons.

**Descends from.** ERA Real Estate suitcase, 100% billboard viable

**Copy.**

> # Your health, all in one place.
> Consultations, prescriptions, nutrition, wearables and your score.
> 
> Pura knows your body like you do.

**Placement.** Sheikh Zayed Road unipole. Read at 100kph from 80 metres.

**Layout.** Message left third, visual right two thirds. Tagline beneath the headline at roughly a quarter of its size, logo opposite. Wireframe at `sketches/B1-layout.png`.
